Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

AuraAlarm is built and run by Okeke Chiemeria John, based in Nigeria. This page explains what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, and a hashed (not readable) version of your password.
  • Alarms and settings: alarm times, schedules, sounds, labels, and preferences, so they sync across your devices.
  • Uploaded files: custom ringtones, loved-one photos, and bank payment receipts if you send them.
  • Location (optional): only if you create a location reminder and allow access. We use your coordinates to trigger the arrival alert. We do not track you continuously in the background or sell location data.
  • Plan details: trial end date, VIP status, and referral counts.
  • Usage analytics: page views and actions such as sign-ups and alarms created, measured with Google Analytics 4. We do not send your name, email, or user ID to Google Analytics.

2. How we use it

To run your account, ring and sync your alarms, send alerts you set up, confirm payments, keep the service secure, improve the app, and answer your messages. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Security

All communication uses encrypted HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed. Your data is stored on our backend and is not end-to-end encrypted, so the site operator can technically access it. I limit that access to running and supporting the service.

4. Advertising and cookies

AuraAlarm uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. Analytics may also use cookies. We use local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings.

5. Where data is stored

The website is delivered by Google Firebase Hosting. Account data is stored on a Render server with a PostgreSQL database. These providers may process data outside Nigeria.

6. Payments

VIP can be paid by bank transfer and is reviewed manually. We do not store card numbers. We may keep payment records (amount, date, status) for accounting and legal reasons, even after an account is deleted.

7. Deleting your account

Go to Settings, then Account & Data, then Delete my account. This removes your profile, alarms, location reminders, and uploaded photos from our database. You can also email okeke12347@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy of your data.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, object to certain processing, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Other people's information

If you upload someone else's photo or details, you must have their permission.

10. Children

AuraAlarm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

12. Contact
