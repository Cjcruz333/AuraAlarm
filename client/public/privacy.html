Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

At AuraAlarm ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible via https://auraalarm.web.app, your privacy is fundamental. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of information we collect, how it is used, and how you have full control over your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your chosen username, email address, and a securely salted bcrypt hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Alarm & Preference Data: We store the alarm times, repeat schedules, selected sounds, timezone preferences, and custom labels you create so they sync across your devices.
  • Uploaded Files: If you upload custom ringtones, loved one photos, or bank payment receipts, these files are stored on our application server.
  • Location Data (Optional Geofences): When you choose to set location-based arrival reminders, your browser requests permission to access your device coordinates. Coordinates are used solely to trigger proximity alert chimes when you arrive at your specified destination. We do not continuously track your location in the background or sell your location data to third parties. You can revoke location access at any time in your device or browser settings.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All communication between your device and our servers is strictly transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed. Note that standard cloud data is stored on our secure backend and is not end-to-end encrypted in a manner that precludes server processing.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

  • Google Analytics 4: We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate, anonymous metrics (such as page visits and feature engagement) to improve application performance. No personally identifiable information is transmitted to Google Analytics.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure: AuraAlarm is hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google Cloud) for front-end delivery and Render for backend API processing with PostgreSQL storage.

4. Payments & Subscriptions

Optional Aura Pro memberships can be purchased via direct bank transfer or manual review. When you submit payment proof (such as a bank transaction reference or receipt image), it is reviewed manually by the site administrator. We do not store credit card or debit card numbers on our servers.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have complete ownership of your data. You may delete your account at any time directly within the application:

How to Delete Your Account:

Navigate to Settings → scroll to Account & Data → tap Delete my account. This immediately and permanently erases your user profile, alarms, location reminders, uploaded photos, and activity logs from our database.
