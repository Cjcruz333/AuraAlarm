About AuraAlarm

A smart, reliable web alarm clock, world clock, and wellness platform designed for restorative sleep and mindful mornings.

What is AuraAlarm?

AuraAlarm is a progressive web application (PWA) built to provide dependable alarms, customizable wake-up tones, world clocks across major timezones, calming ambient soundscapes, and arrival reminder geofences.

Core Features

  • Smart Alarm Clock: Set recurring alarms, custom snooze intervals, and personalized wake-up sounds.
  • World Clock: Live multi-timezone clocks for global coordination.
  • Sleep Aid Soundscapes: Calming ambient audio, white noise, and relaxing frequencies to help you fall asleep peacefully.
  • Arrival & Destination Alerts: Geofence reminders that alert you upon arriving at destinations like work, gym, or home.
  • Stopwatch & Timers: High-precision lap timers and countdowns for workouts and daily routines.

Technology & Open Standards

AuraAlarm is built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, the Web Audio API, Service Workers for offline asset caching, and an Express/Node.js backend. All data transmission between your browser and our servers is secured with HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Important Note on Web Alarms

Mobile browsers (iOS Safari and Android Chrome) enforce battery optimizations and security restrictions that may mute background audio or silence tabs when locked. For critical wake-up events, we always advise keeping your phone plugged in with the app open, or using your device's built-in native clock as a primary backup.
