About AuraAlarm

AuraAlarm is a web and Android alarm clock with custom wake-up sounds, personal messages for loved ones, a world clock, sleep sounds, timers, and arrival reminders.

Who built it

I'm Okeke Chiemeria John, a developer based in Nigeria. I built AuraAlarm to make waking up feel more personal, with a greeting from someone you care about instead of a cold buzzer. It is a small independent project, and I read every message people send me.

Contact and support: okeke12347@gmail.com
Portfolio: okeke-john-portfolio.web.app

How it works

AuraAlarm is built with React, a Node.js backend, and a PostgreSQL database. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. It is not end-to-end encrypted, and the Privacy Policy explains exactly what is stored and why.

Important note about alarms

Phones limit what web apps can do in the background, especially iPhones. For anything important, keep AuraAlarm open with your phone plugged in, and always keep your phone's built-in alarm as a backup.
